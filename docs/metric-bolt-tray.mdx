## Capacity and fit

| Bolt | Bore diameter | Holes per length | Total holes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M3 | 4 mm | 14 | 126 |
| M4 | 5 mm | 10 | 90 |
| M5 | 6 mm | 7 | 63 |
| M6 | 7 mm | 5 | 45 |

Each bore provides **0.5 mm radial clearance** and a **0.4 mm entry chamfer**. For a standard bolt, select the row by measuring from under the head to the tip. Wide flanges and washers may occupy adjacent spaces.

## Print settings

- **Dimensions:** 219 × 201 × 53.2 mm. Print at 100% scale with the holes facing up.
- **Nozzle:** 0.8 mm. The raised lettering and guides share the same top layers.
- **Two-color 3MF:** Assign a filament to the tray body and another to the aligned markings.
- **Manual swap with STL:** Use 0.4 mm layers, including the first. Change filament after the layer ending at 52.4 mm and before the first marking layer ending at 52.8 mm.
